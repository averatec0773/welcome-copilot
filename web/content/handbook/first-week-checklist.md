Welcome! This checklist walks you through your first five days. Your manager
will schedule a kickoff call on day one — everything below is self-serve.

## Before day one

Confirm you received three emails: your welcome email, a Google Workspace
invitation, and a Paylocity enrollment link. If any are missing, write to
it@mentella.example.

## Day 1 — accounts

Sign in to your @mentella.example Google account and set up 2-step
verification (required within 48 hours). Complete your Paylocity profile:
direct deposit, tax forms, and emergency contact.

## Day 2 — EHR access

Submit the EHR access request form linked from the IT Hub. Access is granted
within one business day. You cannot see any client information until your
supervisor countersigns the access request — this is a HIPAA requirement,
not a formality.

## Day 3 — scheduling

Connect your Calendly to your Mentella Google Calendar and set your working
hours. Your intake coordinator assigns your first clients only after your
calendar shows availability.

## Day 4 — compliance training

Complete the HIPAA basics module (about 45 minutes) and the documentation
standards module. Both are assigned in your training portal.

## Day 5 — meet your pod

You'll join your regional pod's weekly sync. Come with questions — the pod
is your first stop for day-to-day questions after this handbook.
