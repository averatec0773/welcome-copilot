IT support at Mentella runs through the IT Hub. This page covers what's
issued, how to connect securely, and how fast to expect a response.

## The IT Hub

The IT Hub is the single portal for every IT request: new equipment, the
EHR access request form, password resets, and general tickets. Bookmark it
during your first week — most IT questions in this handbook route back to
a form there rather than a direct email.

## Standard-issue setup

New hires receive a laptop, a headset, and a monitor, preconfigured with
your @mentella.example Google Workspace account, VPN client, and EHR
shortcut before day one. If anything is missing, or you need a second
monitor for a home setup, submit an equipment request through the IT Hub —
standard requests ship within 3 business days.

## VPN and secure wifi

Connect to Mentella's VPN any time you're accessing the EHR or Google
Workspace from outside a secured office network — this includes home wifi.
Never connect to the EHR over public wifi (coffee shops, airports, hotels)
even with the VPN on; if you must work from a public network, tether to
your phone's hotspot instead.

## Reporting phishing

Forward any suspicious email to it@mentella.example, or use the "Report
Phishing" button in Gmail if you see it — don't click links or reply
first. Mentella occasionally runs internal phishing simulations; reporting
a simulation is treated the same as reporting a real one, with no penalty
either way.

## Response times

Standard IT Hub tickets, including password resets and access requests,
are handled within 1 business day. Anything marked urgent — a locked
account blocking client-facing work, a broken VPN, a live phishing report —
gets a same-business-day response. If you haven't heard back inside those
windows, reply to your open ticket rather than filing a new one.
