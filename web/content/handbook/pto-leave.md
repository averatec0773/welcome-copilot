This page covers how PTO accrues, how to request time off, and Mentella's
sick day, holiday, and parental leave policies.

## Accrual

Full-time clinicians accrue 15 PTO days per year, credited at 1.25 days per
completed month, up to a cap of 22.5 days banked at any time. Part-time
staff accrue on a prorated basis tied to scheduled hours. Unused PTO up to
5 days carries over into the next calendar year; anything above that is
paid out or forfeited per your offer letter.

## Requesting time off (2 weeks notice, cover your calendar first)

Request PTO in Paylocity at least 2 weeks in advance for anything longer
than a single day. Before you submit the request, block the dates in
Calendly and either cancel or reassign any clients already booked in that
window — approval can be delayed if your calendar still shows open
availability during requested time off. Same-day or next-day PTO for
personal emergencies doesn't need the 2-week notice; just notify your pod
lead as soon as you can.

## Sick days

Sick days are a separate bucket from PTO: 6 days per year, accrued from day
one, no advance notice required. Notify your pod lead before your first
scheduled session that day, and reschedule or reassign any clients on your
calendar. Sick time doesn't need to be requested in Paylocity in advance —
log it the same day or the next business day.

## Holidays

Mentella observes 8 paid company holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day,
Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, the day after
Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. Client-facing staff aren't scheduled on
these dates; Calendly automatically blocks them once your working hours
are set.

## Parental leave overview

Mentella offers 12 weeks of paid parental leave for the primary caregiver
and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver, in addition to any leave you're
entitled to under applicable state or federal law. This is an overview
only — contact people-ops@mentella.example as soon as you know a leave is
coming so they can walk you through eligibility, timing, and how it
coordinates with PTO.
