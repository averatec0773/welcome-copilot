Mentella runs payroll through Paylocity. This page covers the pay schedule,
direct deposit, reading your stub, and getting reimbursed.

## Pay schedule (semi-monthly)

Mentella pays semi-monthly: on the 15th and on the last business day of
each month, covering the two halves of that month. If the 15th falls on a
weekend or holiday, pay moves to the prior business day. Salaried
clinicians see the same gross amount each pay period; hourly and
per-session staff are paid based on hours or sessions logged and approved
in the prior period. Your first paycheck arrives on the first scheduled
payday after your start date, paid out by direct deposit once you set up
direct deposit in Paylocity — if you start mid-period, that first
paycheck is prorated to the days actually worked.

## Setting up direct deposit

Set up direct deposit in your Paylocity profile during your first week —
it's one of three items on the day-1 checklist alongside tax forms and your
emergency contact. You can add up to 3 accounts and split pay by percentage
or fixed amount. Changes to direct deposit take one full pay cycle to take
effect, so update it early if your bank is changing.

## Reading your pay stub

Your stub in Paylocity breaks gross pay into base salary or session pay,
any stipends (CE stipend, on-call pay), and a separate line for
reimbursements paid that cycle. Deductions are grouped into taxes,
benefits, and any retirement contribution. If a number looks wrong, check
the pay period dates at the top first — pay stub questions are usually a
timing mismatch, not an error.

## Reimbursements

Submit reimbursement requests through Paylocity's expense module within 30
days of the expense, with a receipt attached — requests without a receipt
or past 30 days need people-ops approval to process. Approved
reimbursements are paid out on the next regular pay date, shown as a
separate line on your stub rather than as a standalone payment.

## Contact (people-ops@mentella.example)

For direct deposit issues, missing pay, pay stub questions, or
reimbursement status, email people-ops@mentella.example. Include your
employee ID from Paylocity and the pay period in question — it's the
fastest way to get a specific answer rather than a general one.
