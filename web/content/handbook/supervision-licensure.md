Clinical supervision and license compliance are both non-negotiable at
Mentella. This page covers what's provided and what's on you.

## Weekly supervision

Every clinician meets with their assigned clinical supervisor for one hour
per week, scheduled through Calendly like any other recurring appointment.
Supervision is protected time — it should never be the slot you cancel to
fit in a client. Missed sessions must be rescheduled within the same week,
not skipped.

## Licensure renewals are your responsibility

Mentella verifies your license is active at hire and re-verifies annually,
but tracking your own renewal date and completing it on time is your
responsibility, not the practice's. The credentialing team sends a
reminder email 90 days before your license expiration as a courtesy, not a
guarantee — a lapsed license means an immediate pause on seeing clients
until it's renewed.

## Multi-state practice rules

You may only see a client while they are physically located in a state
where you hold an active license, or where you're authorized to practice
via an interstate compact Mentella participates in. This is checked at
intake based on the client's stated location, but it's your responsibility
to confirm at the start of any session where a client mentions travel —
location, not billing address, is what governs which state's rules apply.

## CE stipend

Mentella provides a $500 annual stipend toward continuing education,
reimbursed through the same Paylocity expense process as other
reimbursements. CE credits count toward the stipend whether they're
required for your renewal or elective; submit your certificate of
completion along with the reimbursement request within 30 days of the
course.

## Adding a new state

To add a new state to your credentials, email people-ops@mentella.example
with the state and your license number once you've applied. Credentialing
typically takes 60-90 days depending on the state board, and you cannot see
clients located in that state until credentialing confirms you're cleared —
plan ahead if you know a client may be relocating.
